GaRuRu Thai Izakaya (4.7 stars, 89 reviews)

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
* Some inconsistency in food quality was noted, with complaints about overly salty clam soup and a long wait for the crab/shrimp cakes.
* One customer complained that the oyster omelet was inedible and expressed disappointment when the staff refused to return or exchange it.
* A tiny minority of customers expressed overall dissatisfaction with the customer service and food quality.

## Location and Parking
* Located at Sukhumvit Soi 20, Bangkok.
* Highly accessible and within easy walking distance from both Asoke and Phrom Phong BTS stations.
* There is no on-site parking lot at the restaurant, but reviews mention free parking is available nearby.

## Price
* Most customers reported an average spending of ฿200 to ฿800 per person.
* Generally considered reasonable and offering good value for money, though one negative review criticized it as expensive.

## Service
* The staff are widely praised for being exceptionally friendly, polite, attentive, and welcoming, making guests feel at home.
* As a newly opened venue, some reviewers noted that food preparation can occasionally require a bit of patience.
* There was a complaint regarding service recovery where the staff refused to exchange a dish because the customer had already tasted a spoonful.

## Other
* Features both indoor dining spaces and an outdoor garden/terrace area.
* Many guests discovered this hidden gem through Google reviews, social media recommendations, or by simply passing by.
* A staff member named Joe was specifically commended for his outstanding friendliness and hospitality.
