# English Summary

## Name
Jae Bank (4.9 stars, 35 reviews), located at the Emsphere branch in Bangkok.

## Recommendations / Strengths
- Serves authentic, rich, and spicy Northeast Thai (Isan) cuisine.
- Highly praised signature dishes include Som Tam (papaya salad), grilled pork neck, various grilled meats, and grilled catfish.
- The restaurant features a lively and fun atmosphere, complemented by retro music and live band performances.

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
- Some reviewers noted that certain dishes have small portions with lower value for money.
- The music inside can be quite loud and noisy.
- A few specific items, such as the papaya salad or minced meat salad, did not appeal to every reviewer.

## Location and Parking
- Conveniently situated on the 5th floor of the Emsphere shopping mall in downtown Bangkok.
- Easily accessible with parking and wheelchair-accessible facilities available in the mall.
- Suitable for both group gatherings and solo dining, with little to no waiting time reported by most.

## Price
- Average spending ranges roughly between ฿200 and ฿1,400 per person.
- Due to the prime mall location, some customers feel the prices are slightly high or offer average value.

## Service
- The staff is described as enthusiastic, friendly, attentive, and helpful with menu recommendations.
- The owner is also very kind, receiving high praise for overall service quality.

## Other
- Spice levels can be customized to personal preferences, making it friendly for those who cannot handle extreme spice.
- Open through the evening, making it a great spot to relax with a beer.

## Comparison with Other Restaurants
None

## Brief Summary
This is a lively and vibrant restaurant inside a Bangkok mall specializing in authentic Northeast Thai cuisine. It is ideal for friends and diners who enjoy spicy food, energetic music, and a fun atmosphere, though those seeking larger portions or quiet surroundings may want to consider other options.
