## 名稱
台北萬豪酒店 Garden Kitchen（4.1 顆星、1,447 篇評論）

## 推薦/優點
*   甜點表現極為出色，尤其是馬卡龍、檸檬塔與現做的熔岩巧克力蛋糕。
*   用餐環境優雅且採光佳，擁有大型落地窗與挑高綠意景觀。
*   食材精緻且品質穩定，推薦松葉蟹、鹽烤鮭魚、法式洋蔥湯與現做牛肉湯。
*   自助吧動線安排妥當，且有獨特的蜂巢蜂蜜與大塊乾酪供賓客使用。

## 不推薦/缺點
*   熱食與主餐的品項種類較少，不適合追求「多樣化品項」的食客。
*   飲品部分被認為有待加強，評論建議應增加現榨果汁。
*   服務生收盤過於頻繁，有時會在中斷客人交談時顯得不夠周到。
*   部分評論認為海鮮表現普通，且某些時段缺乏烤牛肉等經典主食。

## 地點停車
*   位於萬豪酒店一樓，交通便利，搭乘捷運或開車皆可。
*   設有地下停車場，車道寬敞。
*   停車折抵規則為消費每滿 1,000 元折抵 1 小時（部分評論提到最多可折抵 4 小時）。

## 價位
*   平日午間 Semi-buffet 價位約在 800 至 1,600 元之間，主餐單點則更高。
*   搭配國泰世界卡買一送一或其他旅展優惠券時，CP 值被評為極高。

## 服務
*   整體服務專業有禮，帶位時會詳細介紹環境。
*   補餐與收盤速度非常即時。
*   偶有評論反應服務生在收盤時缺乏觀察力，會干擾嚴肅討論或插話。

## 其他
*   餐廳氣氛安靜，非常適合商務洽談、約會或多人聚餐。
*   平日午間可選擇不點主餐、僅享用自助吧，內容已足夠充裕。

## 和其他餐廳比較
*   與同類型的大型自助餐廳（Buffet）相比，餐點種類明顯較少，但勝在用餐環境更佳、食材更精緻。

## 簡短總結
Garden Kitchen 是一款適合追求精緻度而非豐富度的半自助餐廳，特別推薦給熱愛甜點與安靜環境的人。若能搭配信用卡優惠，將會是非常具備 CP 值的五星級飯店用餐體驗。

# English Summary

## Name
Garden Kitchen at Taipei Marriott Hotel (4.1 stars, 1,447 reviews)

## Recommendations / Strengths
*   Exceptional dessert selection, specifically highlighting the macarons, lemon tarts, and molten chocolate cake.
*   Elegant dining atmosphere with high ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows, and a relaxing garden view.
*   High-quality and fresh ingredients, including snow crab, grilled salmon, and French onion soup.
*   Well-organized buffet layout with unique features like fresh honeycomb and large cheese wheels.

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
*   Limited variety of hot dishes and main course options compared to traditional large-scale buffets.
*   Beverage quality could be improved, with suggestions to include fresh fruit juices.
*   Overly frequent plate clearing by staff which can sometimes interrupt private conversations.
*   Occasional disappointment regarding the absence of certain staples like roast beef during specific shifts.

## Location and Parking
*   Conveniently located on the 1st floor of the hotel, accessible by car or MRT.
*   Spacious underground parking is available.
*   Parking is validated at 1 hour per NT$1,000 spent, with some reports of a 4-hour maximum.

## Price
*   Weekday semi-buffet prices range from approximately NT$800 to NT$1,600; ordering main courses increases the cost.
*   The value for money (CP value) is considered excellent when using credit card promotions (e.g., buy-one-get-one) or travel fair vouchers.

## Service
*   Staff are generally professional, polite, and provide thorough introductions to the dining area.
*   Replenishment of food and plate clearing is very efficient and prompt.
*   A few reviewers noted that staff could be more mindful of timing when approaching tables to avoid interrupting guests.

## Other
*   The quiet and sophisticated atmosphere makes it ideal for business meetings, dates, or small group gatherings.
*   On weekdays, guests can opt for the salad bar only without ordering a main course.

## Comparison with Other Restaurants
*   Compared to typical large-scale hotel buffets, Garden Kitchen has fewer items but offers a superior environment and higher ingredient refinement.

## Brief Summary
Garden Kitchen is a premium semi-buffet ideal for diners who prioritize quality and ambiance over sheer variety. It is a top choice for dessert lovers and those seeking a quiet setting, especially when utilized with promotional offers.
