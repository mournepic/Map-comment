* 海鮮品質不穩，部分評論提到螃蟹肉質乾扁或不夠新鮮。
* 部分熱食被反映像冷凍半成品加熱，與五星級飯店定位有落差。

* 位於大直萬豪酒店 1 樓，鄰近美麗華百貨與捷運站。
* 飯店地下停車場位子充足且寬敞，但停車折抵時數（約 2.5 至 3 小時）常被認為不夠充裕。

## 簡短總結
適合重視用餐環境、追求主餐與甜點品質，且持有優惠折扣的客群。如果您是追求海鮮種類多樣化或生魚片愛好者，此店可能不符合期待。

# English Summary

## Name
Garden Kitchen (4.1 stars, 1,469 reviews) - Taipei Marriott Hotel 1F.

## Recommendations / Strengths
* Main courses are highly praised, especially the bone-in ribeye steak, lamb chops, and seafood platter.
* The dessert section is a major highlight, featuring exceptional lemon tarts, macarons, and molten chocolate cake.
* The salad bar is fresh and high-quality, including a large cheese wheel for self-serving.
* The dining environment is elegant and bright, with high ceilings and floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking a garden.

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
* The buffet variety is limited, and there is a notable absence of sashimi.
* The beverage section is disappointing, with low-quality juices and a lack of fresh options.
* Seafood quality can be inconsistent; some guests reported dry or less-than-fresh crabs.
* Certain hot dishes felt like reheated frozen processed foods, failing to meet five-star expectations.

## Location and Parking
* Located on the first floor of the Marriott Hotel in Dazhi, near Miramar and the MRT station.
* Ample underground parking is available, but the validated parking time (2.5-3 hours) is often criticized as insufficient.

## Price
* Weekday afternoon tea is around $800-$1,100, while lunch with a main course ranges from $1,400-$2,000.
* Excellent value for money when using credit card discounts (e.g., Cathay World Card 50% off) or travel fair vouchers.
* Without discounts, many feel the price is too high relative to the limited buffet selection.

## Service
* Staff members are generally friendly and professional, with very efficient plate clearing.
* The balloon artist is highly popular among families with children.
* Some diners noted that staff can be overly eager to clear plates, occasionally interrupting conversations.

## Other
* Operates on a semi-buffet model (main course + salad bar), with mandatory main course ordering on weekends.
* Afternoon tea menus change seasonally (e.g., Thai-themed cuisine).
* The atmosphere is quiet and upscale, suitable for business meetings, birthdays, or dates.

## Comparison with Other Restaurants
* Compared to major buffets like Eat Together (Xiang-Shi-Tian-Tang) or Harbour, the variety of food is significantly smaller.
* While it lacks the sheer volume of choices found elsewhere, it offers a more refined and peaceful dining environment.

## Brief Summary
Suitable for diners who value atmosphere, high-quality main dishes, and desserts, especially when using discounts. It is not recommended for those seeking a massive seafood variety or traditional sashimi selections.
