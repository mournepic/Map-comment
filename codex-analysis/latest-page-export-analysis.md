# 中文總結

## 名稱
Karun Thai Tea EmQuartier分店（4.2顆星、223 篇評論）

## 推薦/優點
* 泰式奶茶與冰沙口感綿密順滑、茶香濃郁，且杯頂灑有精緻金箔，非常適合拍照打卡。
* 提供高度客製化的甜度選擇（如 20%、40%），還能更換為燕麥奶或豆奶等植物奶。
* 黑糖蒟蒻片、脆片和鮮奶油等配料表現出色，能大幅提升整體口感與層次。

## 不推薦/缺點
* 許多顧客反映冰塊加得太多，導致實際的飲品分量偏少。
* 若選擇完全無糖，茶味會顯得過於苦澀；而標準甜度對不喜甜的人來說依舊偏甜。
* 有顧客遇到招牌金箔缺貨，店家卻未在點單前主動告知，讓人感到失望。

## 地點停車
* 位於 EmQuartier 百貨 M 樓（靠近 COS 與 Valentino），與 BTS 輕軌站直接相連，交通極為便利。
* 可停放於百貨公司的付費或免費停車場，但假日車位較難尋找。
* 店內空間較小、座位非常有限且容易擁擠，多數時候較適合外帶。

## 價位
* 屬於高端奢華定位的泰茶，價格偏高（小杯約 85-100 泰銖，大杯約 110-125 泰銖）。
* 店內刷卡消費設有最低 200 泰銖的門檻，且曾有大鈔無法找零的情況。

## 服務
* 服務評價非常兩極；部分店員會說中文與英文，點單迅速且態度友善。
* 有不少顧客投訴部分店員態度冷漠、摔杯子、對顧客嘆氣，甚至在表定關店時間前 10 分鐘就急忙收攤拒絕接單。

## 其他
* 店家提供高質感的玻璃瓶裝奶茶，據店員表示可保存一個月，非常適合託運帶回國當伴手禮。
* 促銷期間出示星宇航空飛機票（店員拍照確認）可享有 9 折優惠。

## 和其他餐廳比較
* 與知名品牌手標奶茶（Cha Tra Mue）相比，雖然價格貴了一倍，但包裝更奢華、茶味更濃郁，且能提供更精準的甜度客製化。
* 部分顧客認為其口感與精緻度超越了 Pang Cha 與街頭小店，但也有人覺得味道差不多，不值得溢價購買。

## 簡短總結
本店非常適合想在百貨公司內舒適歇腳、喜愛濃郁茶香與精緻包裝，且偏好客製化低甜度泰奶的旅客。但若您追求高 CP 值、不願排隊，或對店員的服務品質有較高期待，則可能不符合預期。

# English Summary

## Name
Karun Thai Tea (EmQuartier branch) (4.2 stars, 223 reviews)

## Recommendations / Strengths
* The Thai tea and slushies are highly praised for their smooth, rich, and aromatic texture, beautifully topped with elegant gold foil.
* Customers appreciate the highly customizable sweetness levels (ranging from 20% to 70%) and alternative milk options like soy or almond milk.
* Unique toppings, such as brown sugar konjac jelly, cookie crumbs, and whipped cream, add excellent texture and layer to the drinks.

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
* Multiple reviewers complained that the cups contained too much ice, resulting in very little actual tea.
* Ordering 0% sweetness makes the tea taste too bitter, while the standard sweet version can be overwhelmingly sweet for some.
* Some customers experienced disappointments when key toppings, like the signature gold foil, were out of stock without prior notice before ordering.

## Location and Parking
* Conveniently located on the M Floor of the EmQuartier shopping mall (near COS and Valentino), directly connected to the BTS Skytrain.
* Parking is available in the mall's parking lot, though finding a space can be challenging during busy weekends.
* The physical shop is quite small with limited seating, making it crowded and often more suitable for takeout.

## Price
* Positioned as a premium luxury Thai tea brand, with prices on the higher end (small cups around 85-100 THB, large cups around 110-125 THB).
* Credit card payments require a minimum consumption of 200 THB, and the staff sometimes struggle to make change for large bills.

## Service
* Customer service reviews are highly polarized; some staff speak English and Chinese, offering quick and polite assistance.
* However, there are notable complaints regarding rude behavior from specific staff members, such as sighing, slamming cups, and refusing orders 10 minutes before the official closing time.

## Other
* The shop offers high-quality bottled tea in glass jars that can be preserved for up to a month, making them perfect for souvenirs.
* Showing a Starlux Airlines boarding pass yields a 10% discount during the promotional period.

## Comparison with Other Restaurants
* Compared to the popular Cha Tra Mue, Karun is about double the price but offers a richer tea aroma, premium packaging, and superior sweetness customization.
* Some reviewers prefer Karun over Pang Cha for being less sweet and more balanced, though a few customers feel the taste is similar to street-side vendors and not worth the premium.

## Brief Summary
This spot is perfect for travelers looking to enjoy a premium, rich, and visually stunning Thai tea with customizable sweetness in a luxury mall setting. It may not be suitable for budget-conscious visitors, those who dislike queues, or those who expect consistent hospitality during peak hours.
