# English Summary

## Name
Triangle Point Stir-fry (三角點熱炒) (4.2 stars, 4,917 reviews).

## Recommendations / Strengths
* The sautéed pig liver is a must-order, praised for its crispy exterior and tender interior.
* Other recommended dishes include stir-fried Basella (Chuanqi) with sesame oil, stir-fried noodles, and steamed grouper.
* Extremely fast service with complimentary self-service rice and ice.
* The dining area is bright, clean, and spacious, making it ideal for large groups.

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
* The "Three-cup" series and fried dragon balls received negative feedback for being unsatisfying or too floury.
* Salted egg tofu is often too dry, and the ginger intestines can be tough and hard to chew.
* Inconsistency in following dietary requests, such as adding chili despite explicit "no spicy" instructions.
* Occasional hygiene issues like fruit flies or overwhelming air freshener scents from the restroom.

## Location and Parking
* Conveniently located near the Banqiao Flower Market.
* The venue is very popular and often crowded even on weekdays; reservations or calling ahead for takeout is advised.
* The environment is noisy and lively, with some smoke drifting in from the entrance.

## Price
* Average spending ranges from NT$400 to NT$800 per person, which is considered affordable.
* High value for money, especially with the "all-you-can-eat" white rice policy.

## Service
* Service is generally efficient and fast, though staff attitude varies.
* Some guests reported staff refusing to help with photos or failing to note specific cooking instructions.

## Other
* Offers a wide selection of beverages and alcoholic drinks.
* Features semi-open seating and large tables suitable for groups of 9 or more.

## Comparison with Other Restaurants
Reviews mention that this location was formerly "Lin Yang Gang (臨洋港)"; some long-time patrons feel the quality was better under the previous management.

## Brief Summary
A classic, affordable stir-fry spot perfect for casual gatherings and those who enjoy bold flavors and fast service. It is best for those who love signature dishes like pig liver, but less suitable for diners seeking a quiet or highly attentive experience.
