* 生魚片冰櫃的拉門設計被多次反應不便於夾取。
* 部份時段熱門食材（如龍蝦、干貝）排隊時間較長或補貨速度較慢。

* 位於竹北喜來登飯店內，近新竹高鐵站，交通十分便利。
* 飯店地下室停車位充足，格子大且好停。

# English Summary

## Name
Feast Buffet - Sheraton Hsinchu Hotel 2F (3.9 stars, 3,262 reviews).

## Recommendations / Strengths
* The dessert section is the ultimate highlight, with Canelés, Macarons, and fresh Crepes receiving high praise.
* Weekend specials are highly recommended, featuring lobster soup, sashimi-grade scallops, and teppanyaki scallops.
* Features a unique Indian cuisine station and a dedicated Muslim-friendly dining area.
* Offers a wide variety of ice creams, including Haagen-Dazs, Mingo, and local Hsinchu Fuyuan Peanut ice cream.

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
* Hot food quality is inconsistent; fried shrimp tempura is often criticized for being cold or having thick, soggy batter.
* Some meat dishes, like roasted beef, can be dry, and seafood like crab is occasionally reported as lacking freshness.
* The design of the sashimi cabinet doors is noted to be awkward and difficult to use while holding a plate.
* Long queues or slow refills for high-end items like lobster and scallops during peak hours.

## Location and Parking
* Located within the Sheraton Hsinchu Hotel in Zhubei, providing easy access near the Hsinchu High-Speed Rail station.
* Ample basement parking with large spaces is available.
* Diners enjoy up to 4 hours of free parking validation, which is very convenient for drivers.

## Price
* Afternoon tea via vouchers costs around $600-$800, while lunch and dinner range from $1,200 to over $2,000.
* Excellent value (high CP) when using credit card "Buy One Get One" deals or travel fair vouchers.
* Regular prices for weekend dinner are considered expensive by some relative to the food variety.

## Service
* Table clearing is very efficient, and the staff are generally proactive and polite.
* Convenient table-side payment service is provided to avoid queues at the front desk.
* Some complaints mention confusing check-in procedures and minor communication barriers with foreign staff.

## Other
* Regularly hosts themed food festivals, such as Korean, Thai, or Indian months.
* Features a dedicated kid-friendly food area and offers generous discounts for seniors over 70.
* The dining environment is spacious and well-organized compared to many other crowded buffets.

## Comparison with Other Restaurants
* Often compared to "Eat Together" (饗食天堂); most feel Feast has superior desserts but fewer hot food choices.
* Considered more comfortable and less crowded than "Sunrise" (旭集).
* Reviewers noted this branch performs significantly better than the Sheraton Taoyuan branch.

## Brief Summary
Feast is ideal for dessert lovers and those who prioritize a comfortable, relaxed dining environment, especially when using discounts. It may disappoint those paying full price who expect a vast array of premium seafood or highly refined hot dishes.
