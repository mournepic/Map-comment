## 名稱
Quartier Foodhall / Gourmet Eats（4.4 顆星、3,344 篇評論），位於曼谷 EmQuartier 購物中心 B 樓。

## 推薦/優點
- 匯集眾多泰國知名街頭小吃與米其林必比登推薦品牌，如紅大哥水門雞飯（粉紅雞飯）、陳牛記雲吞麵、奈安釀豆腐等。
- 餐點選擇多元，涵蓋泰式料理、印度現烤烤餅與咖哩、日式餐點、素食專區以及特色甜點（椰子冰淇淋、耀華力路烤麵包、芒果糯米飯）。
- 用餐環境寬敞現代且冷氣涼爽，桌面與餐具清潔衛生（提供紫外線或獨立包裝餐具），旁邊超市亦提供生鮮現買代客烹調服務。

## 不推薦/缺點
- 平日午餐尖峰時段人潮擁擠且吵雜，常有顧客以個人物品佔位導致一位難求。
- 部分攤位餐點份量偏小且價格偏高，亦有少數評論反映品質不穩（如炒河粉調味淡、雞肉偏乾冷或衛生偶有瑕疵）。
- 部分攤位店員英語溝通能力有限；預付儲值卡若未使用完畢需注意有效期限與退款手續。

## 地點停車
- 位於曼谷素坤逸核心地段的 EmQuartier 商場地下一樓，直通 BTS Phrom Phong 站，交通十分便利。
- 商場內設有大型停車場、自動地下停車及代客泊車服務，消費可折抵停車費（通常享前兩小時免費），但尖峰時段車位較難尋。

## 價位
- 平均每道餐點或人均消費約 100 至 300 泰銖，相較於路邊攤與傳統美食街偏高。
- 考量到高級商場的舒適環境、衛生條件與空調設施，多數顧客認為價格合理且物有所值。

## 服務
- 出餐效率普遍快速，部分熱門攤位配備取餐呼叫震動器。
- 清潔人員清理桌面迅速勤快，但個別攤位人員服務態度較為一般或偶有出錯餐點情況。

## 其他
- 支援多元付款方式，包括美食街專用卡、Rabbit 悠遊卡、信用卡、Line Pay 及 QR 掃碼支付；使用現金儲值卡離場前需至櫃檯退餘額。
- 空間友善且設施齊全，具備電梯、無障礙動線並提供兒童安全高腳椅。

## 和其他餐廳比較
- 相較於 Terminal 21 的 Pier 21 美食街，此處價位高出約一倍以上，但環境更為高級精緻、乾淨且聚集較多名店與異國料理。
- 相較於暹羅百麗宮（Siam Paragon），此處規模稍小但非尖峰時段相對容易找到座位。

## 簡短總結
適合重視用餐環境衛生、想在舒適冷氣房內一站式品嚐泰國知名米其林街頭小吃與異國料理的遊客與上班族。不適合追求極致便宜路邊攤價格、大份量餐點或討厭尖峰擁擠佔位人潮的消費者。

# English Summary

## Name
Quartier Foodhall / Gourmet Eats (4.4 stars, 3,344 reviews), located on the B Floor of EmQuartier Shopping Mall, Bangkok.

## Recommendations / Strengths
- Features renowned Thai street food vendors and Michelin Bib Gourmand awardees, such as Go-Ang Pratunam Chicken Rice, Nai Ouan Yentafo, and Sawang Noodles.
- Offers a diverse selection of cuisines, including local Thai dishes, authentic Indian curries with tandoori naan, Japanese meals, vegetarian options, and popular desserts (coconut ice cream, Yaowarat toasted buns, mango sticky rice).
- Provides a clean, modern, and well-air-conditioned dining environment with hygienic utensils and a nearby supermarket offering cook-to-order services.

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
- Gets heavily crowded and noisy during weekday lunch peak hours, where table hogging with personal items is common.
- Some stalls serve relatively small portions for premium prices, with occasional feedback regarding inconsistent flavor or food temperature.
- Some stall staff have limited English proficiency, and prepaid food cards require attention to expiration and refund policies.

## Location and Parking
- Prime location in the basement of EmQuartier, with direct pedestrian access from BTS Phrom Phong station.
- Ample parking facilities within the mall, including automated and valet parking with free parking validation (e.g., first 2 hours free), though finding spots can be difficult during peak hours.

## Price
- Typical spend ranges from 100 to 300+ THB per person, higher than standard street stalls and budget food courts.
- Most diners consider the pricing fair given the upscale mall location, hygiene, and comfortable amenities.

## Service
- Food preparation is generally quick, with some stalls providing electronic buzzer paging systems for pick-up.
- Cleaning staff clear and sanitize tables promptly, though counter service at individual stalls can occasionally be indifferent or prone to minor order mistakes.

## Other
- Multiple payment options are supported, including Rabbit Card, credit cards, Line Pay, QR prompt payments, and prepaid food court cash cards (refunds must be claimed before leaving).
- Family-friendly and accessible, featuring baby high chairs, elevators, and wheelchair accessibility.

## Comparison with Other Restaurants
- Compared to Terminal 21's Pier 21, Quartier Foodhall is noticeably more expensive, but it offers a more upscale, cleaner ambiance with higher-end vendor selections.
- Compared to Siam Paragon, it is slightly more compact but generally easier to find seating during off-peak times.

## Brief Summary
Quartier Foodhall is ideal for shoppers, office workers, and tourists looking for a clean, air-conditioned venue to enjoy famous Michelin-recognized Thai street eats and global dishes in one spot. It is less suited for budget-conscious travelers seeking cheap street-food prices or large portions during busy peak hours.
