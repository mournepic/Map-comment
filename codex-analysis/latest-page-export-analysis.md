## 名稱
Emporium 美食廣場 / Gourmet Eats（4.3 顆星、966 篇評論）

## 推薦/優點
- 匯集多家米其林必比登推薦攤位與泰國知名街頭小吃名店，餐點選擇豐富多樣。
- 用餐環境明亮整潔且設有冷氣，靠窗座位可俯瞰本查西里公園與城市美景。
- 提供泰式、日式、印度菜、清真料理與素食餐點，各式泰式甜點與冰品也大受好評。
- 餐點品質與衛生狀況佳，深受外國遊客與當地上班族喜愛。

## 不推薦/缺點
- 部分評論抱怨餐點份量偏少、味道較為普通或品質不穩定（如麵條未煮熟、血豆腐過乾硬）。
- 平日午餐高峰時段人潮擁擠，一位難求且餐桌清理較不不及時。
- 有少數顧客反映部分攤位服務人員態度冷漠、動作遲緩或忽視顧客。

## 地點停車
- 位於 Emporium 百貨公司內（4樓/5樓），與 BTS 澎蓬站（Phrom Phong）直接相連，交通極為便利。
- 百貨公司設有付費停車場，亦有顧客提及可善用 90 分鐘免費停車時限快速用餐。

## 價位
- 平均每人消費約 100 至 400 泰銖不等。
- 價格比一般路邊攤高出約 1.5 到 2 倍，但考量到百貨環境與食品衛生，大多數人認為性價比高。

## 服務
- 清潔人員維護環境頻率高，整體服務效率尚可。
- 評價呈兩極化，部分攤位員工親切熱情，但也有顧客抱怨服務態度差或付款溝通不順。

## 其他
- 支援多元付款方式，可使用 BTS 兔子卡（Rabbit Card）、信用卡、LINE Pay 或預付卡。
- 美食廣場旁邊即為 Gourmet Market 高檔超市，吃完飯後方便順便購物。

## 和其他餐廳比較
- 與 Terminal 21 的 Pier 21 美食廣場相比，這裡價格較高，但環境更為乾淨舒適、座位寬敞且不那麼擁擠。
- 與 EmQuartier 或 Siam Paragon 相比，這裡人潮相對較少，用餐氣氛更加悠閒。

## 簡短總結
本美食廣場非常適合重視用餐衛生、舒適冷氣環境，並想一次品嚐多家米其林與泰國名店小吃的遊客與家庭。對於追求極致平價街頭價格，或是想避開平日午餐人潮的顧客則較不適合。

# English Summary

## Name
Gourmet Eats / Emporium Food Hall (4.3 stars, 966 reviews)

## Recommendations / Strengths
- Features a wide variety of famous Thai street food stalls and Michelin Bib Gourmand-recommended vendors.
- Offers a clean, bright, air-conditioned environment with window seats overlooking Benchasiri Park.
- Provides diverse dining options including Thai, Japanese, Indian, Halal, vegetarian dishes, and popular Thai desserts.
- Maintains high hygiene standards and fresh ingredients, highly favored by tourists and locals alike.

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
- Some reviews mention small portion sizes, ordinary taste, or inconsistent quality (e.g., undercooked noodles or dry pork blood).
- Can get crowded during weekday lunch peak hours, making it difficult to find seating and leading to delayed table clearing.
- Occasional customer complaints regarding cold, slow, or inattentive service at specific stalls.

## Location and Parking
- Located inside Emporium Shopping Mall (4th/5th floor), directly connected to BTS Phrom Phong Station for easy access.
- Parking is available in the mall, with reviewers mentioning convenient quick meals within parking limits.

## Price
- Average spending ranges from ฿100 to ฿400 per person.
- Prices are roughly 1.5 to 2 times higher than traditional street stalls, but most customers consider it reasonable for a luxury mall setting.

## Service
- Cleaning staff work consistently to keep the seating areas and tables tidy.
- Customer service feedback is mixed; while many staff are described as polite and fast, others were criticized for slow service or bad attitudes.

## Other
- Supports multiple payment options, including BTS Rabbit Card, credit cards, LINE Pay, and prepaid cash cards.
- Conveniently located adjacent to the Gourmet Market supermarket for grocery shopping after dining.

## Comparison with Other Restaurants
- Compared to Terminal 21's Pier 21, it is slightly more expensive but offers a cleaner, quieter, and much more comfortable atmosphere.
- Compared to EmQuartier and Siam Paragon, it is generally less crowded and easier to secure seating.

## Brief Summary
This food hall is ideal for tourists, families, and shoppers who prioritize food hygiene, air conditioning, and a great selection of Michelin-rated Thai foods. It is less suitable for budget travelers seeking bottom-dollar street prices or those trying to avoid peak lunch hour crowds.
