# 中文總結

## 名稱
大屯自然公園（4.6 顆星、375 篇評論）

## 推薦/優點
- 海拔約 800 公尺，夏季氣溫比市區低 3 至 7 度，為絕佳的避暑勝地。
- 擁有大屯池湖景與群山環繞的自然景致，常有雲霧繚繞，風景如畫且靜謐療癒。
- 環湖木棧道與步道平緩好走，設有無障礙通道與多座涼亭野餐區，適合輕鬆散步。
- 生態極為豐富，四季可欣賞杜鵑花、櫻花、野薑花、水生植物及多種鳥類蝴蝶。

## 不推薦/缺點
- 園區內部分木棧道或湖面橋樑偶有維修整修情形，可能需繞道通行。
- 往返二子坪的連接階梯較為陡峭且易生青苔，雨天濕滑容易跌倒。
- 園區部分路段樹蔭遮蔽較少，夏季正午時曝曬感較強烈。
- 假日人潮眾多時車位一位難求，服務站內未提供飲水機及販賣部。

## 地點停車
- 位於新北市三芝區巴拉卡公路旁，可搭乘 108 公車至二子坪站再步行前往。
- 設有一號與二號停車場，僅支援電子票證感應收費（如悠遊卡），不收現金。
- 假日容易客滿需要排隊碰運氣，平日造訪則停車便利且清幽。

## 價位
- 園區免費對外開放，無需門票費用。
- 汽車停車費為每半小時 10 元，收費親民；評論提及一號停車場機車可免費停放。

## 服務
- 遊客服務站提供旅遊資訊與生態解說諮詢。
- 站內提供維護良好的五星級乾淨洗手間。
- 園區有管理單位巡查，遇違規釣魚或破壞生態行為會予以制止。

## 其他
- 遊客中心開放時間為每日 09:00 至 16:30（每月最後一個週一及農曆除夕休館）。
- 設有開闊草皮，環境友善寵物，適合帶毛孩外出踏青。
- 秋冬季傍晚天黑迅速（約 18:00 後完全暗下），若較晚前往建議自備照明設備。

# English Summary

## Name
Datun Nature Park (4.6 stars, 375 reviews)

## Recommendations / Strengths
- Situated at an elevation of around 800 meters, it is 3 to 7 degrees cooler than downtown Taipei, making it an excellent summer retreat.
- Boasts stunning volcanic basin scenery, a serene pond with mountain reflections, and ethereal misty atmospheres.
- Features flat and gentle boardwalks with barrier-free access, resting pavilions, and grassy picnic areas suitable for easy walking.
- Rich in biodiversity, offering sightings of native azaleas, cherry blossoms, wild ginger flowers, birds, and aquatic wildlife across different seasons.

## Not Recommended / Weaknesses
- Wooden boardwalks and footbridges over the pond occasionally undergo maintenance, requiring detours.
- Stone stairs connecting to the Erziping Trail can be steep and mossy, posing slip hazards during wet weather.
- Low-lying vegetation offers limited shade in certain open sections, leading to intense sun exposure on hot afternoons.
- Parking spots fill up rapidly on weekends, and the visitor station does not provide water dispensers or concession stands.

## Location and Parking
- Located along the Balaka Highway; accessible by taking Bus 108 to the Erziping stop followed by a 10- to 15-minute walk.
- Parking Lots 1 and 2 operate exclusively via electronic payment (such as EasyCard), with no cash accepted.
- Parking spaces are severely limited and crowded on weekends, while weekday parking is generally easy and smooth.

## Price
- Park admission is completely free of charge.
- Parking is inexpensive, charging around NT$10 per 30 minutes for cars, with some reviews noting free scooter parking at Lot 1.

## Service
- The visitor center provides trail information and nature educational materials.
- Clean and well-maintained public restrooms (praised as five-star quality) are available on-site.
- Park administration staff actively patrol the area to prevent unauthorized activities like fishing.

## Other
- The visitor center is open daily from 09:00 to 16:30 (closed on the last Monday of each month and Lunar New Year's Eve).
- Wide grassy areas make the park dog-friendly and great for relaxing with pets.
- Daylight fades very rapidly after sunset in autumn and winter (pitch dark by 18:20), so bringing a headlamp or flashlight is advised for evening visits.

## Comparison with Other Restaurants
None

## Brief Summary
Datun Nature Park is an idyllic, cool, and easily walkable destination ideal for families, seniors, pet owners, and anyone seeking a refreshing summer escape in nature. It is not suitable for those looking for bustling commercial facilities, dining vendors, or visitors unwilling to navigate tight weekend parking.
