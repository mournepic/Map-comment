大屯遊客服務站

📍服務台桌上
⭐️5顆紀念章

大屯遊客服務站位於大屯自然公園內，如果想前往遊客服務站，必須先穿過整座自然公園。園區內有生態池、步道及豐富的自然景觀，其中生態池的池水十分清澈，天氣好的時候甚至能看見池底，是個很適合散步放鬆的地方。

遊客服務站內除了介紹大屯山周邊的地質、生態與自然環境之外，也展示了不少動植物標本。值得一提的是，由於大屯山區過去氣候潮濕，非常適合馬藍生長，因此曾是北臺灣重要的藍染原料產地之一。馬藍加工後可製成藍靛，作為藍染的重要染料，因此館內也特別介紹了當地與藍染文化之間的關係。

紀念章放置於服務台桌上，共有5顆可以收集。來到這裡不妨放慢腳步，在欣賞自然景觀之餘，也順便認識大屯山的人文與產業歷史。

Datun Visitor Center

📍On the service counter desk
⭐️ 5 souvenir stamps

Datun Visitor Center is located within Datun Nature Park. To reach the visitor center, visitors must first walk through the park itself. The area features ecological ponds, walking trails, and abundant natural scenery. One of the highlights is the ecological pond, whose water is remarkably clear—on sunny days, you can even see the bottom of the pond. It is a wonderful place for a relaxing stroll and to enjoy the natural surroundings.

Inside the visitor center, exhibits introduce the geology, ecology, and natural environment of the Datun Mountain area. There are also numerous specimens of local plants and animals on display. One particularly interesting aspect of the exhibition focuses on Strobilanthes cusia (commonly known as Chinese indigo). Due to the region’s humid climate, the Datun area was once one of the most important sources of indigo-dye plants in northern Taiwan. After processing, the plant could be used to produce indigo dye, making it an essential material in traditional blue-dyeing practices. As a result, the visitor center also highlights the historical connection between the area and Taiwan’s indigo-dyeing culture.

The souvenir stamps are placed on the service counter, with five designs available to collect. If you visit, it’s worth slowing down for a while—not only to enjoy the natural beauty of the park, but also to learn more about the cultural and industrial history of the Datun Mountain region.

有位爬山姐姐告訴我，因為她長的像野鴉，所以叫野鴉椿。但我無論是上看下看左看右看，實在無法看出她是隻野鴉。原來是果實未開時像野鴉停棲在樹梢，而枝葉揉碎後有類似「臭椿」的味道，合起來就是「野鴉椿」。
